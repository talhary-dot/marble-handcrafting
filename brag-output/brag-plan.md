# Brag Plan: Sang Tarash Atelier

## What is this app?
Sang Tarash is an ultra-luxury bespoke stonecraft and marble atelier creating hand-carved monolithic basins, fluted travertine pedestals, and architectural stone art sculpted from unbroken geological strata.

## The angle
In a world of mass-manufactured composite decor and disposable interiors, Sang Tarash crafts monolithic pieces out of solid mountain stone that outlive civilization. High-end architectural luxury meets master Afghan stonecutting heritage.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep obsidian darkness erupts with the geological texture of the stone quarry and an authoritative statement: *"Carved from living mountains."*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Mountain & Master Masonry**: Geological quarry strata transitioning into hand-chiseling and chisel-cut perfection.
- **The Iconic Monoliths**: Triple showcase of flagship creations—The Fluted Travertine Pedestal ($340), The Carved Stone Vessel ($260), and The Carrara Totem Stand ($380).
- **The Luxury Acquisition Standard**: Crated in reinforced timber, certificate of authenticity, white-glove delivery, and zero-compromise solid natural stone guarantee.

## Outro / punchline
The Sang Tarash gilded emblem locks in place with the definitive motto: *"Sculpted to outlive civilization."* Followed by the atelier call to action: `sangtarash.com`.

## User flow worth showing
Patron examines geological provenance and hand-carved finish → Explores edition details and stone varieties → Secures certified acquisition with bespoke white-glove delivery.
